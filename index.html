Seller · Atomic Offers

Accepting an offer creates an on-chain ZB-1 atomic lock. Once the lock confirms, the seller cannot cancel or manually transfer the NFT through this client until settlement or expiry.

Connect Noir Wallet to load offers.

Buyer · Atomic Checkout

Payment opens only after the seller lock is chain-verified. The selected buyer completes two Noir payments: Step 1 protocol fee, Step 2 seller payout. The site verifies both Zcash transactions automatically.

Your locked atomic purchases will appear here.
Locked-sale rule: seller chooses exactly one buyer and chain-locks the NFT first. The buyer then pays the 3% protocol fee and 97% seller payout with Noir. After both transactions reach finality, ZB-1 ownership resolves automatically to that buyer. Seller has no post-payment approve/reject step.
Noir-only checkout: no Zkool, no ZIP-321, no TXID paste. Step 1 sends exactly 3% to the protocol treasury. After that fee confirms, Step 2 sends the remaining 97% to the seller. The NFT settles automatically only after both payments are verified.

List ZEC BLOCK

Listings are signed marketplace intents mirrored across multiple public relays. Website updates do not erase the listing; the client reconstructs it from relay events. Listing does not transfer the NFT or move ZEC.

Make Offer

Offers move no ZEC. Seller selects one buyer and chain-locks the NFT first. Only the selected buyer can then pay with Noir.

Transfer ZEC BLOCK

Transfers are irreversible protocol events. Verify the recipient commitment carefully before approving the Zcash transaction.
