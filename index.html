List ZEC BLOCK

Listings are signed marketplace intents mirrored across multiple public relays. Website updates do not erase the listing; the client reconstructs it from relay events. Listing does not transfer the NFT or move ZEC.

List ZEC BLOCK for USDC

Pay and receive USDC on Base. Your Noir identity signs the ZB-1 listing intent, while your connected Base wallet receives the seller payout. Listing requires one Base transaction. A 3% marketplace fee applies when the item sells.

List ZECS for USDC

Verified balances. Noir signs the ZECS identity challenge, the marketplace verifies your available ZECS, and your Base wallet creates the order. ZECS remains on Zcash; only USDC payment settles on Base.

List ZECS for ZEC

0% protocol fee. Your Noir transparent address receives 100% of the listed ZEC price. Buyer pays once; ZECS balance updates after the Zcash payment is confirmed.

Request Purchase

Fixed-price request. The buyer cannot change the seller's listing price. This request moves no ZEC. If the seller accepts, the NFT is locked to this buyer for the checkout window, then the selected buyer pays 3% protocol + 97% seller.
